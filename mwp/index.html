About
MWP

Man with A Plan brings expertise, finesse and innovation to the table when it comes to planning one of the most memorable days of your life. Be it a grand wedding, a milestone celebration or an anniversary – Man with A Plan handcrafts each celebration with utmost love and care.

They assist with all the planning, facilitation, negotiation, and handling of various aspects of wedding planning to ensure the best services at the most competitive price. The end-to-end solutions come to you with the ‘Man with A Plan Guarantee’, backed by their strong in-house production set-up and long-established vendor relations in the industry.

Founder

A serial entrepreneur with vast experience planning weddings, festivals and conventions over the past 8 years, Jaideep Singh is the founder of Man with A Plan.

As one of the key partners in the International Convention of Wedding Fraternity, the biggest premium wedding fraternity convention held on this side of Asia, Jaideep has in-depth knowledge about the landscape of Indian weddings.
