4. Location Data

Pulse only accesses your device's location at the moment you choose to set your status to "Office." At that moment, the app checks whether your device is within approximately 100 meters of your employer's registered office location (geofencing), in order to confirm the Office check-in. Pulse does not access or track your location when you set your status to "Work From Home" or "Site," and does not track your location continuously or in the background. You will be prompted for permission by your device's operating system before any location data is collected, and declining that permission will prevent Office check-in from being confirmed.
