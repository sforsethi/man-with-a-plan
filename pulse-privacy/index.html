Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [Company Legal Name] ("Pulse", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with the Pulse mobile application and related services (the "Service"), an attendance and employee time-tracking tool used by organizations and their employees.

1. Who This Policy Covers

Pulse is typically deployed by an employer ("Organization Account") for use by its employees. In most cases, the Organization Account is the "data controller" for employee attendance data, and Pulse acts as a "data processor" or service provider carrying out instructions from the Organization Account. This Policy describes our practices as both the provider of the Service and, where applicable, as a processor of data on behalf of Organization Accounts.

2. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

  • Name, work email address, employee/role identifier, and organization affiliation.
  • Login credentials or authentication tokens.

Attendance and work data

  • Clock-in and clock-out timestamps, shift schedules, and attendance status.
  • Device location at the time of check-in/check-out, where location-based verification is enabled by the Organization Account.
  • Check-in photos, if photo verification is enabled by the Organization Account.

Device and usage information

  • Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • App diagnostic and crash data, and general usage/interaction data within the app.

4. How Information Is Shared

  • With your employer (Organization Account): attendance and time data collected through Pulse is made available to the Organization Account that provisioned your account, for payroll, scheduling, and workforce management purposes.
  • With service providers: we may share information with vendors who provide infrastructure, hosting, analytics, or customer support services on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information where required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business transfers: information may be transferred in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain attendance and account data for as long as the Organization Account maintains an active subscription, and afterward for a period necessary to comply with the Organization Account's legal, tax, and labor record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including encryption of data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Because attendance data is typically controlled by your employer (the Organization Account), we may direct requests relating to that data to your employer, or fulfill them jointly. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Pulse is intended for use by working adults through their employer and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International Data Transfers

Information may be stored and processed in countries other than the one in which you or your Organization Account are located. Where required, we rely on appropriate legal mechanisms to safeguard such transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
