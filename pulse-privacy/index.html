Pulse

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CPM India Sales and Marketing Private Limited ("Pulse", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with the Pulse mobile application and related services (the "Service"), an employee app used to keep track of whether employees are working from home, from the office, or from a site.

1. Who This Policy Covers

Pulse is deployed by an employer ("Organization Account") for use by its employees. Your employer creates your account on the backend and provides you with your login credentials — you do not register or sign up for Pulse yourself. In most cases, the Organization Account is the "data controller" for employee data, and Pulse acts as a "data processor" or service provider carrying out instructions from the Organization Account.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

Your account (name, work email or username, and organization affiliation) is created by your employer, not by you. We do not collect any sign-up information directly from you.

Work status data

  • The work status you set each day — Work From Home, Office, or Site — and the timestamp of that entry.
  • Device location, only at the moment you set your status to "Office." This is used solely to confirm, via geofencing, that your device is within approximately 100 meters of your employer's registered office location. We do not collect location data when you set your status to "Work From Home" or "Site," and we do not track your location continuously or in the background.

We do not collect any other personal information from you — no contacts, photos, browsing activity, biometric data, or continuous location history.

4. How Information Is Shared

  • With your employer (Organization Account): your work status data is made available to the Organization Account that created your account, for workforce management purposes.
  • With service providers: we may share information with vendors who provide infrastructure or hosting services on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information where required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business transfers: information may be transferred in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain attendance and account data for as long as the Organization Account maintains an active subscription, and afterward for a period necessary to comply with the Organization Account's legal, tax, and labor record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including encryption of data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Because attendance data is typically controlled by your employer (the Organization Account), we may direct requests relating to that data to your employer, or fulfill them jointly. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Pulse is intended for use by working adults through their employer and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International Data Transfers

Information may be stored and processed in countries other than the one in which you or your Organization Account are located. Where required, we rely on appropriate legal mechanisms to safeguard such transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
